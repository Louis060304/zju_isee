2.1 Schottky contact to n-type silicon ( Φ M > Φ S ) Φ M > Φ S (Phi_(M) > Phi_(S))\left(\Phi_{M}>\Phi_{S}\right)

    半导体内部的 E 0 , E C E 0 , E C E_(0),E_(C)\mathrm{E}_{0}, \mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{C}} 和 E V E V E_(V)\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{V}} 连同 E F E F E_(F)\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{F}} 一起向下移动。
  • At the metal-semiconductor interface, however, E 0 E 0 E_(0)E_{0} must be continuous. This causes E 0 E 0 E_(0)\mathrm{E}_{0} in the semiconductor near the interface to bend upwards to join up with E 0 E 0 E_(0)\mathrm{E}_{0} in the metal.
    然而，在金属-半导体界面处， E 0 E 0 E_(0)E_{0} 必须连续。这导致半导体界面附近的 E 0 E 0 E_(0)\mathrm{E}_{0} 向上弯曲，以与金属中的 E 0 E 0 E_(0)\mathrm{E}_{0} 连接。

    接触后，来自硅的电子（多数载流子）流入金属中较低的能态（以使费米能级在热平衡时保持恒定）。它们在硅中留下未补偿的施主离子，形成耗尽区。

    当对金属-硅结施加反向偏压 V R V R V_(R)\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{R}} 时，硅中电子的势垒增加。硅中的电子不再能流入金属。

  • The current that flows through the reverse-biased junction is therefore small.
    反向偏置结点流过的电流因此很小。
  • When a forward bias V a V a V_(a)\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{a}} is applied to the metal-silicon junction, the potential barrier to electrons in the silicon is reduced. Many electrons in the silicon can surmount the reduced barrier to flow into the metal.
    当对金属-硅结施加正向偏压 V a V a V_(a)\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{a}} 时，硅中电子的势垒降低。许多硅中的电子能够克服降低的势垒流入金属。

    因此，金属-半导体结表现为整流结，类似于 p-n 结二极管。它被称为肖特基二极管。

    但是请注意，电流仅由多数载流子（在本例中为电子）的流动引起。没有少数载流子参与。

    接触后，费米能级通过电子从金属向硅的转移而对齐。
    → E C → E C rarrE_(C)\rightarrow \mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{C}} and E V E V E_(V)\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{V}} in the bulk (interior) move upwards with E F E F E_(F)\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{F}} relative to their positions at the metal-silicon interface.
    → E C → E C rarrE_(C)\rightarrow \mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{C}} 和 E V E V E_(V)\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{V}} 在体内（内部）相对于它们在金属-硅界面处的位置，随着 E F E F E_(F)\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{F}} 向上移动。
    → → rarr\rightarrow the surface of the silicon becomes more n-type.
    → → rarr\rightarrow 硅的表面变得更具 n 型特性。
    → → rarr\rightarrow there is no depletion region in the silicon.
    → → rarr\rightarrow 硅中没有耗尽区。
  • There is no barrier for electrons in the silicon to move towards the metal, and only a fairly small barrier for electrons to move from the metal to silicon.
    硅中电子向金属移动没有势垒，电子从金属向硅移动只有相当小的势垒。

Ohmic contact of n-type silicon ( Φ M < Φ S ) Φ M < Φ S (Phi_(M) < Phi_(S))\left(\Phi_{M}<\Phi_{S}\right) under bias
n 型硅的欧姆接触 ( Φ M < Φ S ) Φ M < Φ S (Phi_(M) < Phi_(S))\left(\Phi_{M}<\Phi_{S}\right) 在偏压下

  • When a positive voltage is applied to the metal wrt the silicon, electrons can easily flow “downhill” from the silicon into the metal.
    当金属相对于硅施加正电压时，电子可以很容易地从硅“顺势流”到金属中。
  • When a negative voltage is applied to the metal wrt the silicon, electrons can easily flow over the small barrier from metal to silicon.
    当金属相对于硅施加负电压时，电子可以很容易地越过金属到硅之间的小势垒。
  • Currents can flow readily in both directions between the metal and silicon.
    电流可以在金属和硅之间双向自由流动。
  • Therefore, this junction is an ohmic contact.
    因此，该结是欧姆接触。

3.1.2 Ohmic contact to p-type silicon ( Φ M > Φ S ) Φ M > Φ S (Phi_(M) > Phi_(S))\left(\Phi_{M}>\Phi_{S}\right)
3.1.2 与 p 型硅的欧姆接触 ( Φ M > Φ S ) Φ M > Φ S (Phi_(M) > Phi_(S))\left(\Phi_{M}>\Phi_{S}\right)

  • After contact, the Fermi levels are aligned by the transfer of holes from the metal to the silicon.
    接触后，通过空穴从金属向硅的转移，费米能级得以对齐。
  • → → rarr\rightarrow The surface of the silicon becomes more p-type.
    → → rarr\rightarrow 硅表面变得更具 p 型特性。
  • → → rarr\rightarrow There is no depletion region at the silicon surface.
    → → rarr\rightarrow 硅表面没有耗尽区。
  • There is no barrier for holes in the silicon to move towards the metal, and only a fairly small barrier for holes to move from the metal to silicon.
    硅中空穴向金属移动没有势垒，且空穴从金属移动到硅中只有相当小的势垒。
  • Therefore it is an ohmic contact.
    因此这是一个欧姆接触。

  • For ideal metal-semiconductor contacts :

  • If a metal forms an ohmic contact with one type of silicon (either n or p ), it will form a Schottky contact with the other type. (e.g., aluminium (AI) forms a good ohmic contact with p-type silicon, but forms a Schottky contact with n-type silicon)
    如果一种金属与某种类型的硅（无论是 n 型还是 p 型）形成欧姆接触，那么它将与另一种类型形成肖特基接触。（例如，铝（Al）与 p 型硅形成良好的欧姆接触，但与 n 型硅形成肖特基接触）
  • This means, that we need to use two different metals for making ohmic metal contacts to silicon devices (which contain both p-and n-type regions) on a silicon wafer.
    这意味着，我们需要使用两种不同的金属来制造硅器件的欧姆金属接触（这些器件包含 p 型和 n 型区域）在硅片上。
    → → rarr\rightarrow In practice this is not easy and also increase process complexity.
    → → rarr\rightarrow 实际操作中这并不容易，而且会增加工艺复杂性。

Depletion layer width decreases as the doping concentration in the silicon increases

  • When the m-s contact is forward biased, the potential barrier in the semiconductor is reduced, the electrons can surmount the reduced potential barrier and are injected into the metal.
    当金属-半导体接触正向偏置时，半导体中的势垒降低，电子可以克服降低的势垒并注入金属中。
  • When the m-s contact is reverse biased, electrons from the metal can tunnel through the thin depletion region into the conduction band of the semiconductor.
    当金属-半导体接触反向偏置时，电子可以通过薄的耗尽区隧穿进入半导体的导带。
